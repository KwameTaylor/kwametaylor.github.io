About

I'm a PMP-certified technical program manager based in Los Angeles. For more than eight years I've led social impact programs, building their data systems and managing organizational change as teams adopt new processes and tools. I have a data science background and I write code in Python, Salesforce Apex, HTML, CSS, Java, and JavaScript.

I'm also a graphic designer, creating logos, visual identities, and print work for a range of clients. Outside of work, I build my own coding projects, ROM hacks, and games, and I'm really into video games, Afrofuturism, music, and fashion.
